## 4. Full Store Description (Section 39)
```markdown
Stop switching between open tabs, hunting through fine print, and copy-pasting tables into spreadsheets. 

**Compare Anything – AI Web Comparison** gives you instant, structured, side-by-side analysis across 2 to 4 browser tabs in a single click.

Whether you're shopping for laptops, evaluating SaaS subscription tiers, comparing job offers, or weighing academic programs, Compare Anything extracts real webpage content and generates an objective comparison matrix grounded strictly in verifiable facts.

---

### 🚀 Key Features

• **Zero Hallucination Guarantee:** The AI model is strictly constrained to visible webpage facts. If a spec, price, or policy isn't explicitly stated on the page, it reports "Not stated" instead of guessing.
• **Side-by-Side Matrix:** Clean responsive table comparing 2, 3, or 4 tabs across dynamically identified criteria (pricing, specifications, terms, features, pros/cons).
• **Personalized Priorities:** Tell the AI what matters most to you (e.g., "battery life", "annual billing discount", "remote flexibility") to receive tailored recommendations.
• **Winner & Trade-off Analysis:** Objective "Best Overall" determination plus specific callouts for "Best for Budget", "Best for Power Users", and "Key Differences".
• **Transparent Source Links:** Jump directly back to the original source tab with one click.
• **Export Anywhere:** Download your comparison report as a formatted Markdown or CSV file with one click.
• **Private & Secure:** No background tab tracking, no browsing history logging, and zero user analytics tracking. Data is processed ephemerally and discarded immediately after generation.

---

### 🛠️ How It Works in 3 Simple Steps

1. **Browse:** Open 2 to 4 tabs you want to compare (e.g., two competitor SaaS pricing pages).
2. **Add Tabs:** Click the Compare Anything icon in your toolbar and add the active tab or select open tabs.
3. **Compare:** Enter any custom priorities (optional) and click **"Compare"**. Your side-by-side breakdown is ready in seconds!

---

### 🔒 Privacy & Data Usage (Section 40)

Compare Anything adheres strictly to the Google Chrome Web Store Single-Purpose and Minimal Permissions policies:

- **`activeTab` & `scripting`:** Used strictly when you click "Add Current Tab" to read visible DOM text from the tab you explicitly select. We never inspect tabs running in the background.
- **`storage`:** Used locally to save your recent comparison history and extension preferences on your machine.
- **No Background Monitoring:** Does not run persistent network listeners or track your browsing habits.
- **No Data Monetization:** Your webpage text is never sold, shared with data brokers, or used for AI model training.
- **Backend Protection:** API keys and heavy AI reasoning reside in an encrypted backend proxy; your extension client never exposes sensitive tokens.
