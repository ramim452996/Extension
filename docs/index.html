How It Works

Three steps from tab overload to clear, structured decision making.

1

Add a page

Navigate to your first option and click + Add Page. The extension extracts clean visible text directly from your active tab.

2

Add another

Switch to competitor tabs, job postings, or product pages and add them with one click. Compare up to 4 tabs simultaneously.

3

Compare

Hit Compare to generate an analytical, side-by-side comparison matrix with winner verdicts, trade-offs, and CSV export.
