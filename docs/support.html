Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Why do I see "Not stated" in some comparison cells?
Compare Anything is engineered with strict zero-hallucination rules. If a product warranty, specific price tier, or qualification requirement is not explicitly written on the page you added, the AI is instructed never to guess or make assumptions. It reports "Not stated" to keep your data 100% trustworthy.
2. Can I compare more than 4 pages at once?
Currently, Compare Anything is optimized for 2 to 4 pages. This guarantees optimal readability on standard laptop screens and keeps AI token processing well within rapid response limits (under 5 seconds).
3. Why does the extension say "Cannot compare Chrome internal pages"?
Due to Chrome browser security policies, extensions are not permitted to inspect internal URLs such as chrome://settings, chrome://extensions, or the Chrome Web Store gallery itself. Simply navigate to any standard web page (e.g., e-commerce, documentation, blogs) to add tabs.
4. Does Compare Anything see my private account data or passwords?
No. Password fields, hidden forms, and login tokens are ignored during page text extraction. Furthermore, Compare Anything only extracts text when you explicitly click "Add Current Tab". It does not run background listeners on other tabs.
5. Can I export my comparison?
Yes! At the top right of your comparison results view, click either "Copy Markdown" or "Export CSV" to immediately copy or download the side-by-side data into Notion, Google Sheets, Excel, or GitHub.
