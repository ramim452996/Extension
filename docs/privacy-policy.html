Privacy Policy

Compare Anything – AI Web Comparison

Effective Date: September 9, 2026 | Version 1.0.0

Our Core Privacy Promise: We only process the specific web pages you explicitly choose to add. We do not track your browsing history, run background trackers, sell your information, or store your data long-term.

1. Overview and Single Purpose

Compare Anything is a browser productivity extension designed solely to provide side-by-side comparison tables of 2 to 4 user-selected browser tabs. This Privacy Policy details what minimal data is handled when you use the extension and how your privacy is protected at every step.

2. Information We Access and Process

  • Explicitly Selected Webpage Content: When you click "Add Current Tab", the extension temporarily reads the visible text, title, and URL of that tab. We do not read tabs you do not select.
  • Comparison Priorities: If you choose to enter custom priority text (such as "Focus on pricing" or "Must have USB-C"), that text is processed along with the page content.
  • Local Extension Storage: Your local preferences (such as your backend endpoint URL and optional saved comparisons) are stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local. This data never leaves your computer unless you initiate a comparison request.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ No Browsing History: We do not monitor URLs you visit, search terms, or bookmarks.
  • ❌ No Personal Identifying Information (PII): We do not require an account, email address, name, or phone number.
  • ❌ No Keystroke Logging or Background Scraping: The extension content script runs strictly on-demand.
  • ❌ No Form Autocomplete / Passwords: We strip form inputs, authentication tokens, cookies, and hidden session fields.
  • ❌ No Ad Tracking or Cross-Site Analytics: We do not embed third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook Pixel).

4. Chrome Permissions Justification

To adhere to the principle of least privilege, Compare Anything only requests the minimal permissions required to function:

  • activeTab: Enables the extension to access the title and URL of the active tab only when you interact with the extension popup.
  • scripting: Executes the on-demand text extraction script inside the active tab to retrieve visible content when you click "Add Current Tab".
  • storage: Stores your local configuration and recent comparison history within your browser sandbox.

5. How Data is Sent to AI Providers

When you click "Compare Tabs", the extracted text from your selected pages and your priority prompt are sent securely via TLS/HTTPS to our stateless backend proxy. The proxy securely forwards the prompt to Groq Cloud for language inference under enterprise API zero-retention guidelines.

  • The backend operates statelessly: data is processed in memory and returned immediately.
  • Data is not used to train AI models.
  • Data is not shared with third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

6. Data Retention and Security

Extracted content is stored in your local browser memory only while the popup or comparison view is open. Once you clear your comparison or close the extension, that ephemeral data is discarded. Any cached comparisons stored via chrome.storage.local can be erased at any time by clicking "Clear History" or uninstalling the extension.

7. Children's Privacy

Compare Anything is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect or solicit data from children under the age of 13.
